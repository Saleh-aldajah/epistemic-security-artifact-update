# RQ4(b) open-weight replication — completed but uninformative

RQ4(b) completed 120 episodes with zero voided records. No-defense follows only 1/60 injections; DEI follows 0/60. The qwen3:8b model is therefore too weak to establish susceptibility. It is not evidence of resistance or defense robustness, and it is not used to support the generality claim.

AgentDyn provides the independent generality result: 1,120 episodes with 30 voided attempts excluded, ASR 0.470 → 0.237 (RD −0.232 [−0.286, −0.178], Fisher p=4.76e-16).
