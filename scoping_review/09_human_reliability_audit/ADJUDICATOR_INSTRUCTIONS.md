# Adjudicator Instructions — Wave-1 Disagreement Resolution (44 records)

## Your role
Two domain experts independently coded 114 records; you resolve the 44 on which they
disagreed. For each row, read the title and abstract (full text available on request —
name the token only), then fill exactly two cells: `final_eligibility` and
`final_attack_class`. Your decisions become the study's reference standard, so decide
each record on its own merits against the codebook — you are not scoring the coders.

## What the columns mean
`assessment_1` and `assessment_2` are the two experts' judgments in randomized order per
row; neither side consistently belongs to either expert, by design. You may adopt either
assessment or enter a third answer if the record's own framing supports it. Labels must
be verbatim codebook vocabulary (CODER_BRIEF.md §3–§4 and CODER_INSTRUCTIONS.md):
eligibility ∈ {include-core, include-background, exclude}; attack class = one of the 10
listed classes for any included record, `n/a` only for excluded records.

## Blindness rules
Do not discuss any record with the coders or anyone else before returning the sheet; do
not attempt to identify the coders or look up any project release materials, automated
labels, or prior versions of this study. Judge from the record text and the codebook
alone. Do not reorder, add, or remove rows; fill only the two final columns.

## Return
Return this CSV when all 44 rows are complete. Expected effort: 4–7 hours. Your file is
hash-logged on receipt and released with the study's audit artifacts.
